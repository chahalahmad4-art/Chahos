Chahos is a creative digital agency based in Lebanon, led by Ahmad Chahal. We help businesses turn what they do into a clear online presence and a simpler way to work.

From a gym’s memberships to a clinic’s appointments or a shop’s orders, we design websites and build systems around the people who use them. Brand design, AI and automation connect the experience.
