Your data, clearly explained.

Version: assistant-privacy-v1 · 8 October 2026

Chat and project enquiries are separate

Offline examples run in your browser. When live AI is enabled and you agree, your current question and up to eight recent chat messages are sent to Google Gemini to generate a response. Your conversation stays in this tab’s memory until you clear it or reload; Chahos does not store raw chats in its application logs. The provider may process or retain data according to its account terms. Please avoid sensitive information.

The enquiry form sends only the fields you review: name, email, chosen service and project brief, plus your consent and request reference. When CRM submission is enabled, they are stored in HubSpot for Chahos Agency to respond. Your chat transcript is not included. This does not subscribe you to marketing or reserve an appointment.

Operational records

Server hosting and service providers may keep operational logs under their own terms. The application uses keyed hashes for rate limiting and submission deduplication; the temporary records expire within seven days. Up to 500 application telemetry records contain status, timings and token counts, without raw conversation or contact details.

Access, correction and deletion

Contact Ahmad Chahal on +961 71 727 712 with your request reference to ask about access, correction or deletion of an enquiry. CRM records stay until reviewed and removed by the agency; automatic CRM deletion is not implemented. Never include medical histories, payment details, passwords or confidential documents.
